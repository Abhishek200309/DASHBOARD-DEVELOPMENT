# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard  
A Power BI dashboard providing actionable insights into workforce demographics, promotion readiness, job-level distribution, and employee service patterns.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is designed to help organizations analyze their employee data effectively. It consolidates key HR metrics—such as total employees, gender distribution, service years, distance from office, and promotion eligibility—into a single interactive dashboard. This enables HR teams to make informed decisions around talent management, workforce planning, and employee development.

---

## 🧩 Key Features & Insights

### 🔹 **Total Employees**
- **1470 employees** form the core workforce analyzed in this dashboard.

### 🔹 **Gender Distribution**
| Gender | Count | Percentage |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Male | 882 | 60% |
| Female | 588 | 40% |

Moderate gender diversity exists, with a slightly male-dominated workforce.

---

### 🔹 **Promotion Eligibility**
| Status | Count | Percentage |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Due for Promotion | 72 | 4.9% |
| Not Due for Promotion | 1398 | 95.1% |

Only a small segment is currently eligible for promotion, highlighting the importance of focused upskilling programs.

---

### 🔹 **Employment Status**
| Status | Count | Percentage |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| On Service | 1353 | 92% |
| Retrench | 117 | 8% |

A stable workforce with very low attrition.

---

## 📆 Service Year Breakdown

The dashboard analyzes how long employees have been in the organization:

- **1 year:** 171  
- **2 years:** 128  
- **3 years:** 127  
- **4 years:** 110  
- **5 years:** **196** (largest group)  
- **7–10 years:** 90–110 employees  

A majority fall in the **1–5 year range**, showing a young, growing workforce.

---

## 🪜 Job Level Distribution

| Job Level | Employees |
|-----------|-----------|
| Level 1 | 543 |
| Level 2 | 534 |
| Level 3 | 218 |
| Level 4 | 106 |
| Level 5 | 69 |

Over 70% of employees are in Levels 1 and 2, demonstrating a large entry/mid-level workforce.

---

## 📍 Distance From Office

| Category | Count | Percentage |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Very Close | 229 | 15.58% |
| Close | 301 | 20.48% |
| Far | 940 | 63.95% |

Most employees live far from the workplace—an important factor for transportation planning and hybrid-work policies.

---

## 📝 Conclusion

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** delivers a clear and structured view of the organization's workforce. Key takeaways include:

- Strong workforce stability with low attrition  
- Young employee base with growth potential  
- Balanced gender distribution  
- Significant entry-level workforce  
- Majority of employees living far from work  

These insights are crucial for strategic HR planning, hiring, promotion cycles, training investments, and employee engagement initiatives.
